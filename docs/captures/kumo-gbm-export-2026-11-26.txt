Live text from the unit: the GBM/PRIME probes re-run at the session-72 wrap-up,
and the Kumo logs from this session's runs.  Read-only: the probes open a DRM
node, create one 64x64 AR24 buffer and destroy it; nothing on media is written.
**This is the evidence behind report 275.**

Unit at the capture: Linux `7.3.3 #109 SMP` on hd3 (the session-71 rebaked image),
`pvrsrvkm 56000000.gpu` bound, catacomb running, Kumo **not** running.

The probes are tracked at `tools/bench/gpu/` (`gbmplane.py`, `gbmprime.c`,
`gbmprime2.c`, `ioctllog.c`).  A copy of each was in `/tmp` on the unit.

## What this proves

* WebKit's `AcceleratedSurface` fails at `DMABufBufferAttributes::fromGBMBufferObject`
  because the pvr GBM cannot export the buffer: `plane_count=1` but the plane fd
  is `-1` and there is no GEM handle.  This is the DDK's buffer model, not a Mesa
  policy or a WebKit bug (report 275 §2, §2.2).
* The omapdrm control exports normally, so the probe and the environment are
  sound.
* Earlier "the SGX works on GBM / `EGL_MESA_image_dma_buf_export` is present"
  (reports 267/268) is **not** contradicted: that is the EGL/libdbm path, which
  exports via PRIME from a dumb buffer; WebKit's path allocates through the DDK
  image API and hits the missing handle.

```sh
/tmp/gbmprime2 /dev/dri/card1 rendscan 1     # EBUSY expected: card1 is already mastered
/tmp/gbmprime2 /dev/dri/card0 rendscan 0
```
