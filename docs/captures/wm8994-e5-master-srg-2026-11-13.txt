The reference's playback capture has abe_mcbsp2_dx at mux 7 = safe_mode
(0x000F) and abe_mcbsp2_dr as an input (0x1108): the reference drives neither
data ball while that capture was taken.  Either the capture was not during real
playback, or the codec's DACDAT is not on dx.  Not resolved this session.
