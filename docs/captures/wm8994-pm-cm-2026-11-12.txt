
— the detector still reads `0x0000`.

## 6. The ABE-refclk fix — three states

`CM_ABE_PLL_REF_CLKSEL` = `0x4A30610C`; `CM_CLKMODE_DPLL_ABE` = `0x4A0041E0`;
`CM_IDLEST_DPLL_ABE` = `0x4A0041E4`; `CM_CLKSEL_DPLL_ABE` = `0x4A0041EC`.

| image | 0x4A30610C | 0x4A0041E0 | 0x4A0041E4 | 0x4A0041EC | dmesg lock failures |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| `0x36597040` (previous) | 1 | 0x00000C07 | 0x00000000 | 0x0082EE00 | (not captured) |
| `0x5E97FEBE` (parent only) | 0 | 0x00000C07 | 0x00000000 | 0x0082EE00 | 7 × `clock: dpll_abe_ck failed transition to 'locked'` |
| `0x7C56BDC0` (parent + rate) | 0 | 0x00000C07 | **0x00000001** | **0x00804018** | **0** |

`0x00804018`: M = `(v >> 8) & 0x7FF` = 64, N = `v & 0x7F` = 24 →
38.4 MHz × 64 / 25 = **98.304 MHz**.

Reboot test (image `0x7C56BDC0`): `devmem 0x4A30610C 32 0x00000001`, `reboot -f`,
then after boot `devmem 0x4A30610C 32` → `0x00000000` and
`devmem 0x4A0041E4 32` → `0x00000001` (locked).  The DTS assignment re-applies.

Boot sanity on `0x7C56BDC0`: `wm8994 3-001a: WM8994 revision D`,
`PBR: 40124000.mcbsp <- omap-mcbsp`, `PBR: sound <- playbook-wm8994`,
`Munin touchscreen ready (irq 110)`, display PLL forced, ACM gadget up.
`pbrpmb: topup: BOOTCTR_FCTRY ... = 2e000000` (≥ threshold, no write).
