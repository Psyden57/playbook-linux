MUSB mailbox fault — DRAM ring evidence — 2026-11-19 (session 66)
======================================================================

Two boot attempts of an instrumented stock-mailbox kernel on the
dual-core ARMv7 build. Region 0 was flashed with the marker image,
the unit hung, then a warm reset + flash of the quiet read-back image
preserved the DRAM ring (the chainloader hand-off, trap 321). Raw ring
dumps were read with tools/bench/ringdump.c and decoded with
tools/pb_ring.py. The lines below are verbatim decode output; the ring
is shared by both CPUs via printascii and visibly corrupts some bytes
(trap 332), so hex pointer values may have single-byte damage.
