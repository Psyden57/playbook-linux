3. The two bugs this run fixed
===========================================================================

a) The codec's AIF1 is on McBSP3, not McBSP1/2.  The oracle's routing log,
   the McBSP3 register block (the only active L4_ABE target), and the
   mcbsp3 pad group (0x106/0x108/0x10a/0x10c, mode 1) settle it.  The
   earlier "data ball is abe_mcbsp1_dx" reading was the AIF4/MCBSP1
   route, not the codec.

b) The right channel was silent because the McBSP was programmed mono on
   the first OSS negotiation (XCR2 0x0041, XPHASE clear) and the later
   2-channel hw_params returned early: the driver's guard compared rate
   and width only.  Comparing the channel layout (XPHASE) as well makes
   the second call reconfigure; XCR2 becomes 0x8041 and the right slot
   plays.
